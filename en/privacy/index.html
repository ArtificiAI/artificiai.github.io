Privacy policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Les Services en ligne Artifici inc. (“Artifici”, “we”) takes the protection of your personal information seriously. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect through the artifici.ai website, why we use it, who we share it with and how to exercise your rights. It is based on Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails.

In short

  • You can browse the site without giving us any personal information.
  • Audience measurement cookies (Google Analytics) are only enabled with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • For any question or to exercise your rights, use our contact form (opens in a new tab).

1. Person in charge of the protection of personal information

Artifici’s person in charge of the protection of personal information ensures compliance with this policy and the law. You can reach them at:

Person in charge of the protection of personal information
Les Services en ligne Artifici inc.
Québec (Québec), Canada
Through our contact form (opens in a new tab)

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill out our contact form or write to us, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organization and the content of your message. If you send us a spontaneous application, we also collect the information it contains, such as your résumé.

Information collected automatically

  • Audience measurement, with your consent. If you accept audience measurement cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site: pages viewed, visit duration, referring site, device and browser type, language and approximate region. Google Analytics does not record your IP address.
  • Without your consent, no audience measurement cookie is set. Google may still receive limited technical signals without cookies or persistent identifiers (for example, the fact that a page was viewed), used only to produce overall statistics.
  • Hosting logs. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security and service operation purposes.
  • Your consent choices. Our consent management tool, Axeptio, records your cookie choices so they are respected on your next visits.
  • Your display preferences. The theme (light or dark) and language you choose are stored only in your browser and are not sent to us.

3. Why we use your information

We use your personal information only to:

  • answer your questions and requests, and follow up on our exchanges;
  • assess your application, if you sent us one;
  • understand how the site is used in order to improve it, if you consented;
  • keep the site secure and working properly;
  • meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We make no decision based exclusively on automated processing of your information.

4. Consent

By sending us your information through the form or by email, you consent to our using it for the purposes described above. Audience measurement cookies are only enabled if you accept them in the consent banner. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clicking (also available at the bottom of every page) or by writing to us.

6. Sharing your information and hosting outside Québec

We share your information only with the providers that help us run the site and answer you, and only as needed:

  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): website hosting;
  • Google LLC (United States): audience measurement, if you consent;
  • Axeptio (France): cookie consent management;
  • Microsoft Corporation: contact form (Microsoft Forms);
  • our email provider, for the emails you send us.

Some of these providers store information outside Québec, including in the United States and Europe. Before communicating information outside Québec, we assess whether it will receive adequate protection there, and these communications are governed by contractual commitments.

We may also disclose information when required or permitted by law, for example at the request of a competent authority.

7. Retention

We keep your personal information only as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. It is then securely destroyed or anonymized.

8. Security

We take reasonable security measures, suited to the sensitivity of the information, to protect it against loss, theft and unauthorized access, use or disclosure. Access to your information is limited to the people who need it for their work. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d’accès à l’information and the people concerned, as required by law.

9. Your rights

Subject to the exceptions provided by law, you can:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and obtain a copy;
  • have inaccurate, incomplete or equivocal information corrected;
  • withdraw your consent to its use;
  • ask us to stop disseminating your information or to de-index any hyperlink attached to your name, in the cases provided by law;
  • obtain the information you provided to us in a structured, commonly used technological format.

To exercise these rights, write to the person in charge of the protection of personal information through our contact form (opens in a new tab). We will answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (opens in a new tab).

10. Minors

Our site is intended for organizations and professionals. It is not directed at people under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information about them.

11. Links to other sites

Our site links to third-party sites, such as LinkedIn, GitHub and Microsoft Forms. These sites have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page. If we make a significant change, we will clearly indicate it on the site.

13. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your personal information, write to us through our contact form (opens in a new tab).
